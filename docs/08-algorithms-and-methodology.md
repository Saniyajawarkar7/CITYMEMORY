# 8. Algorithms and Methodology

CITYMEMORY uses historical and rule-based analysis to convert infrastructure records into decision-support evidence.

## Recurrence Analysis
Measures how often similar failures occur and the time between recurring incidents.

## Intervention Survival
Measures the observed time between an intervention and the next related failure. Historical median or average stability can be used for comparison.

## Failure Echo
Identifies historical relationships between failures using spatial proximity, time windows and connected infrastructure. It indicates association, not proven causation.

## Failure Signatures
Finds recurring combinations of contextual factors such as rainfall, season, previous repairs and nearby failures.

## Historical Intervention Comparison
Compares observed outcomes of historically comparable interventions to support evidence-based decisions.

## Preventive Action Planner
Combines recurrence, intervention longevity, failure patterns, infrastructure criticality and cost constraints to rank preventive actions.

## Explainability
Recommendations should show the historical evidence and factors contributing to the score rather than producing an unexplained prediction.

The prototype uses simplified, rule-based methods. Statistical or machine-learning methods can be added when sufficient validated historical data is available.
