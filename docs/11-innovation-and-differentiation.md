# 11. Innovation and Differentiation

## Core Innovation

CITYMEMORY focuses on **institutional memory for urban infrastructure**.

Instead of only recording complaints, repairs or current asset condition, it connects historical failures, interventions and outcomes to support future decisions.

## Key Differentiators

- **Infrastructure Memory:** preserves long-term asset history.
- **Infrastructure Amnesia:** identifies repeated short-lived repair cycles.
- **Failure Echo:** connects historically related failures across assets.
- **Failure Signatures:** identifies recurring failure contexts.
- **Historical Comparison:** uses observed intervention outcomes as evidence.
- **Memory-Weighted Planning:** prioritizes preventive actions using accumulated history.

## What Makes It Different

The innovation is the **integration of historical memory, recurrence, spatial-temporal relationships and intervention outcomes into one decision-support layer**.

CITYMEMORY does not claim to prove causation or predict exact future failure dates. Its primary purpose is to make past infrastructure experience usable for better preventive decisions.
