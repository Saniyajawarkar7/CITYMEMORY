# 12. Impact and Benefits

## Social Impact
- Improves reliability of essential urban infrastructure.
- Supports faster and more informed maintenance decisions.
- Helps reduce repeated infrastructure failures.

## Economic Impact
- Supports better allocation of maintenance budgets.
- Helps identify interventions that historically provided better stability.
- Reduces repeated short-term repair cycles.

## Environmental Impact
- Encourages preventive maintenance instead of repeated emergency repairs.
- Can reduce unnecessary material use and repeated construction activity.
- Supports more efficient infrastructure management.

## Institutional Impact
- Preserves institutional knowledge even when staff or contractors change.
- Creates a structured historical record for future decisions.
- Improves transparency by linking recommendations to historical evidence.

## Long-Term Benefit

CITYMEMORY aims to help cities move from **reactive repair** toward **memory-informed preventive maintenance**.
