# 13. Limitations and Assumptions

## Limitations

- Historical municipal records may be incomplete or fragmented.
- Initial deployment may have limited historical data.
- Inconsistent asset IDs and terminology can affect analysis.
- Historical association does not prove causation.
- The current prototype uses simplified demonstration data.
- Production deployment requires integration with existing municipal workflows.
- Citizen-generated data may require additional privacy and validation controls.

## Assumptions

- Authorized municipal data can be accessed for deployment.
- Asset and intervention records can be standardized.
- Historical outcomes are sufficiently reliable for comparison.
- Municipal staff can validate important recommendations.

## Approach to Limitations

CITYMEMORY is designed for phased deployment, beginning with validated data and a limited infrastructure scope. Recommendations should remain explainable and subject to human review.
