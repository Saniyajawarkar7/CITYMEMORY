# 14. Future Scope

## Failure Forecasting
Use larger historical datasets to estimate future failure risk and likely failure categories.

## Citizen Voice Intelligence
Process citizen text, voice and public feedback to identify recurring infrastructure issues and locations.

## Advanced Spatial Analysis
Use PostgreSQL/PostGIS and infrastructure networks for richer spatial and connected-asset analysis.

## Advanced Analytics
Introduce statistical and machine-learning models when sufficient validated historical data becomes available.

## Municipal Integration
Connect CITYMEMORY with existing complaint, asset, inspection and maintenance systems.

## Multilingual Support
Support multiple Indian languages for citizen feedback and field-level usage.

## Audit and Governance
Add stronger access control, data provenance, recommendation logs and audit trails for production deployment.

## Long-Term Vision

CITYMEMORY can evolve into a continuously learning institutional-memory layer that helps cities preserve experience and make increasingly evidence-based infrastructure decisions.
