# 15. Research and References

## Research Focus

CITYMEMORY is based on the idea that urban infrastructure data should not only record what happened, but also preserve what previous interventions achieved.

The project combines concepts from:

- Urban asset management
- GIS and spatial analysis
- Infrastructure condition monitoring
- Maintenance history
- Recurrence analysis
- Intervention survival analysis
- Decision-support systems

## Research Gap

Many existing systems focus on reporting issues, monitoring assets or managing work orders. CITYMEMORY focuses on connecting **historical failures, interventions and outcomes** so that past infrastructure experience can support future preventive decisions.

- India Urban Data Exchange (IUDX)
- Smart Cities Mission — smartcities.gov.in

- scikit-learn — machine-learning toolkit
- Lifelines — survival-analysis toolkit

## Important Note

The current prototype uses demonstration data and simplified analytical methods. Production deployment would require validated municipal datasets, proper data governance and domain-level evaluation.
