## Research Areas

The project references established work in urban asset management, GIS, preventive maintenance, infrastructure condition monitoring, recurrence analysis and survival analysis.

## Project Position

CITYMEMORY combines these established concepts into an institutional-memory and decision-support workflow for urban infrastructure.

The prototype is a proof of concept and requires validated municipal data and further evaluation before production deployment.
