# Existing Systems

Urban infrastructure platforms commonly support complaint reporting, asset records, GIS mapping, inspections and work-order management.

CITYMEMORY is positioned as a complementary decision-support layer focused on institutional memory: connecting historical failures, interventions and outcomes to support preventive prioritization.

## Comparison Areas

- Complaint and issue reporting
- Asset and work-order management
- GIS-based infrastructure visualization
- Historical intervention tracking
- Intervention outcome memory
- Recurrence and failure-pattern analysis
- History-informed preventive prioritization

## CITYMEMORY Focus

The key distinction is the use of accumulated infrastructure history as evidence for future maintenance decisions rather than treating each incident as an isolated event.
