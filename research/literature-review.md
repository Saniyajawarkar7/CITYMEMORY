# Literature Review

CITYMEMORY draws from established areas of research in infrastructure management and data-driven maintenance.

## Relevant Areas

- Infrastructure asset management
- Predictive and preventive maintenance
- GIS and spatial-temporal analysis
- Infrastructure condition monitoring
- Failure recurrence analysis
- Survival analysis for intervention longevity
- Decision-support systems

## Research Gap

Existing approaches often focus on condition assessment, failure prediction, monitoring or maintenance scheduling.

CITYMEMORY emphasizes a connected **institutional memory layer** that preserves historical failures, interventions and observed outcomes and makes this history usable for preventive decision support.

## Methodological Position

CITYMEMORY does not claim a new machine-learning algorithm. Its innovation lies in combining established analytical methods around a persistent infrastructure-memory model.

The prototype uses simplified methods suitable for demonstration, while advanced statistical or machine-learning methods can be evaluated as more validated historical data becomes available.
